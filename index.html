Hi, I'm Jennifer

I created Ladies Let's Talk Collective because I, like many women, found myself searching for answers—trying to understand the changes happening in my body and not always knowing where to turn.

I'm not a medical professional, but I believe every woman deserves access to honest conversations and trusted information.

That's why I use this space to connect with professionals and bring those conversations to you—so you can feel more informed, more supported, and less alone.
